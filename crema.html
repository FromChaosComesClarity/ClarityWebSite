crema.md

CREMA — the big screen companion

The same library, the same artwork, the same playlists — on a television, driven with a gamepad, from across a room. Nothing to configure and nothing to sync: it is the same app.

01_a_face_for_the_big_screen

Run the same AppImage with --crema, or send it there from the Manager, the bar menu or an autostart entry. Your library is already there.

02_browsing

Made for looking, not managing

  • A home screen with what you played last, a pick for today, and what you have been meaning to get to.
  • Genres, playlists and history as ways through the shelf, not as forms to fill in.
  • Trailers and screenshots on the page, playing while you decide.
  • Achievements from GOG and Steam in one list.
03_the_jukebox

Your music, with a real spectrum

A music player built into the same face: your own folder of music, browsable by artist and album, with a full-screen now-playing view. The visualiser reads the audio that is actually playing rather than animating on a timer.

04_what_it_deliberately_will_not_do

It plays. It does not manage.

Renaming games, scraping artwork, fetching trailers, typing launch commands, uninstalling — none of that is here. It belongs at the desktop mode, where there is a keyboard.

What is left is what you would actually want from a big screen mode: mark a favourite, say you want to play something, add it to a playlist, see achievements, and install a game you own.

⚠ And it never asks for a password

CREMA has no store login. If an account needs signing in to, it says so and points you at the desktop mode — a device-code flow with a password field has no business on a television.
