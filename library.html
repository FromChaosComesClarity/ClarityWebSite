the_library.md

One shelf, everything on it

Games arrive from six different places and stop being six different libraries. That was the whole point.

01_importing

Six kinds of game, one shelf

  • Steam. Your whole account, with playtime and achievements.
  • GOG and Epic. Signed in inside the app, installed without a store client.
  • itch.io, Flatpak and whatever else. If you can run it from a terminal, it can go on the shelf.
  • PICO-8 carts. Hundreds of them, with their own artwork. This alone was worth the trouble.
  • Emulated games. Brought across from EmuLatte and kept in step.

Covers, hero art, logos, descriptions, genres, release years, HowLongToBeat times and review scores get fetched once and kept on your disk. A store changing its API doesn't empty your shelf.

02_the_game_page

Everything about a game, in one place

  • Manuals. GOG ships PDFs with loads of games and never tells anyone. They get downloaded, attached, and read in place.
  • Achievements from GOG and Steam in a single list, with what you've unlocked.
  • Save games backed up to a zip and restored from one, without leaving the page.
  • Trailers, screenshots, and a description actually worth reading.
  • Compatibility. Proton version, prefix, winetricks, environment variables, all per game.
03_your_data

The library is a folder you can carry

Everything the app knows lives in one folder beside the AppImage: the database, every cover and screenshot it fetched, your playlists, your save backups, your settings.

  • Copy it and it moves. Another machine, another drive, a USB stick. It arrives complete.
  • Back it up by copying it. There's no export step, because there's nothing to export.
  • Delete it and it's gone. Nothing left behind anywhere else.
  • No account, no cloud. Nothing gets uploaded, because there's nowhere to upload it to.
04_and_the_awkward_ones

The games that don't just work

Some games need more than a launch command. Those get handled one at a time, as named games with named faults. The things that aren't games at all, like source ports and fan remakes, get their own catalogue.
