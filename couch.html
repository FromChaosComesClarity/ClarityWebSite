couch.md

Couch Mode on the big screen

The same library, the same artwork, the same playlists, now on a television and driven with a controller from across the room. Nothing to configure, nothing to sync. It's the same app wearing different clothes.

01_a_face_for_the_big_screen

Run the same AppImage with --couch, or send it there from the Manager, the bar menu, or an autostart entry. Your library is already waiting.

02_browsing

Made for looking, not managing

  • A home screen with what you played last, a pick for today, and the thing you keep meaning to get to.
  • Genres, playlists and history as ways through the shelf, not forms to fill in.
  • Trailers and screenshots playing on the page while you make up your mind.
  • Achievements from GOG and Steam in one list.
03_the_jukebox

Your music, with a real spectrum

There's a music player built into the same face: your own folder of music, browsable by artist and album, with a full-screen now-playing view. The visualiser reads the audio that's actually playing instead of animating on a timer. Small thing. Makes me happy every time.

04_what_it_deliberately_will_not_do

It plays. It doesn't manage.

Renaming games, scraping artwork, fetching trailers, typing launch commands, uninstalling. None of that is here. It belongs on the desktop, where there's a keyboard.

What's left is what you actually want from a big screen: mark a favourite, say you want to play something, drop it in a playlist, check achievements, install a game you own. Then play it.

⚠ And it never asks for a password

Couch Mode has no store login. If an account needs signing into, it says so and points you back to the desktop. A password field has no business on a television.
