couch.md

Couch Mode on the big screen

The same library, artwork and playlists, on a television, driven with a controller. Nothing to configure, nothing to sync.

01_a_face_for_the_big_screen

Run the same AppImage with --couch, or send it there from the Manager, the bar menu, or autostart.

02_browsing

Made for looking, not managing

  • A home screen: what you played last, a pick for today, the one you keep meaning to get to.
  • Genres, playlists and history as ways through the shelf.
  • Trailers and screenshots playing while you decide.
  • Achievements from GOG and Steam in one list.
03_the_jukebox

Your music, with a real spectrum

Your own folder of music, browsable by artist and album, with a full-screen now-playing view. The visualiser reads the audio actually playing, not a timer.

04_what_it_deliberately_will_not_do

It plays. It doesn't manage.

Renaming, scraping, fetching trailers, launch commands, uninstalling. None of it here. That belongs on the desktop.

What's left is what you want from a big screen: favourite it, add it to a playlist, check achievements, install it. Then play.

⚠ And it never asks for a password

No store login. If an account needs signing into, it says so and points you back to the desktop. A password field has no business on a television.
