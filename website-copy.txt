CLARITY: ALL WEBSITE COPY
=================================

Every word on the site, in one file. Rewrite anything below in your own voice and hand it
back; it goes straight into the pages.

HOW TO EDIT
-----------
  * Change the text UNDER a "###" line. Never change the "###" line itself. That is the
    address the text goes back to.
  * A block can be as many lines as you like. It ends at the next "###".
  * To delete a piece of copy, leave the block empty.

FORMATTING YOU CAN USE
----------------------
  **bold**            *italic*            `code`            [link text](page.html)

Anything else, a <br> or an emoji, just leave as you find it.
    No em dashes, please: a comma, a colon or a full stop instead.

WHAT THE LABELS MEAN
--------------------
  title             the browser tab and Google's headline
  meta:description  the sentence under it in search results
  og:title / og:description   what appears when the link is pasted into chat
  001, 002, ...     a piece of copy on the page, in the order you read it
  h1 h2 h3          headings, largest first
  p                 a paragraph      li  a bullet      figcaption  a caption under a picture
  div span a        short labels, tags and link text

### fixes.html | 012 | div
01_named_faults

### fixes.html | 013 | h3
OutRun 2006: Coast 2 Coast

### fixes.html | 014 | div
A white screen. It seems to hang on the SEGA logo, so it gets killed.

### fixes.html | 015 | p
The intro is a run of video logos, and under a compatibility layer the first can sit there a minute or more. Not frozen, but nobody waits that long. **The intro is now skipped.**

### fixes.html | 016 | p
The community patch installs under a filename Wine replaces with its own, so it never loaded. **The game&rsquo;s own copy now wins**, as on Windows.

### fixes.html | 017 | h3
Resident Evil 2 &middot; Biohazard 2 (Classic REbirth)

### fixes.html | 018 | div
Comes up untranslated and stops dead at a Japanese error box.

### fixes.html | 019 | p
The patch is a graphics library beside the game, and Wine loaded its own instead, so it never ran. **The game&rsquo;s own copy is now loaded first**, with Wine&rsquo;s behind it as a fallback.

### fixes.html | 020 | p
The fallback is the trick: the patch passes what it doesn't implement through to the real library. With nothing behind it, the game crashes instantly.

### fixes.html | 021 | h3
Quake (GOG), the Windows builds

### fixes.html | 022 | div
GLQuake and both mission packs close instantly. No window, no error.

### fixes.html | 023 | p
GOG ships a 3dfx driver from the nineties, and Wine substituted its own, so the game died before a window appeared. **The driver GOG shipped is now used.**

### fixes.html | 024 | h3
Fallout: New California

### fixes.html | 025 | div
&ldquo;Fallout: New Vegas does not appear to be installed.&rdquo; Please insert the DVD.

### fixes.html | 026 | p
Every file downloaded perfectly, but GOG&rsquo;s installer performs three finishing steps afterwards and nothing on Linux did them. **Now applied at launch**, repairing the install you have. No reinstall.

### fixes.html | 027 | h3
Realms of Arkania 1 &amp; 2 &middot; Albion

### fixes.html | 028 | div
DOSBox opens for half a second, then vanishes.

### fixes.html | 029 | p
Two faults. The configuration files GOG&rsquo;s installer copies were never copied, and the game started from the wrong folder, so DOSBox looked one level too high and quit. **Both fixed.**

### fixes.html | 030 | h3
Colony Ship

### fixes.html | 031 | div
Stuck at 50.5% for an hour. Downloading fast. Getting nowhere.

### fixes.html | 032 | p
One file was damaged on a GOG server. Half arrived, the connection closed, every time, and the download kept asking that same server. **Now each piece is checked and a different server is used** when one keeps failing.

### ports.html | 012 | div
01_how_it_works

### ports.html | 013 | h3
the download is yours to fetch

### ports.html | 014 | p
Every entry names the project, links its page and says **what the file is called**: <span class="file">ironwail-0.8.2-win64.zip</span>. Nothing is pulled automatically, so nothing breaks when a project renames an asset.

### ports.html | 015 | h3
the game data comes from your library

### ports.html | 016 | p
Quake's paks, the Doom IWADs, the Build .grp files. Found by probing for the proving file, never by trusting a title, and **linked rather than copied**. The store install is never written into.

### ports.html | 017 | h3
the data is settled before anything is unpacked

### ports.html | 018 | p
&ldquo;You own Quake but it isn't installed&rdquo; is a message you can act on. **A port that installs and then can't start is not.** So the data is resolved first, and the install refuses rather than leave something unplayable.

### ports.html | 019 | h3
a mod download is usually a pack

### ports.html | 020 | p
One 310MB archive held the mod plus twenty-eight extras, and the first file alphabetically was a *voice pack*. Contents are now listed **without unpacking**, largest pre-ticked, and only what you tick is extracted, in load order.

### ports.html | 021 | h3
the engine comes with the mod

### ports.html | 022 | p
No engine installed, one goes in on the same click. One already there is **shared rather than copied**, as the ZDoom family intends. Which Doom a mod runs on is asked **when Play is pressed**, because that's an evening's mood, not an install decision.

### ports.html | 023 | h3
it lands as an ordinary game

### ports.html | 024 | p
Its own name, cover and playtime. Proton, the prefix and every per-game fix apply as they do to a store game, and it joins playlists and genres like anything else.
