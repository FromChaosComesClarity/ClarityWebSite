02_what_it_is.md

One app, two faces, all your games

The Manager is the desktop app: import, organise, scrape, install, launch, with a mouse and keyboard. Couch Mode is the big screen one: fullscreen, gamepad-first, made for a television across the room. Installer works underneath both, pulling your GOG and Epic games down without a store client anywhere in sight.

It's one AppImage. Nothing to install alongside it, and your library, artwork and settings sit in a folder next to it. Portable, backed up by copying it, gone when you delete it.
