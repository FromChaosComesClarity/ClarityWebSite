02_what_it_is.md

One app, two faces, all your games

The Manager is the desktop app: import, organise, scrape, install, launch, with a mouse and keyboard. CREMA is the big screen companion: fullscreen, gamepad-first, made for a television across a room. GRINDER works underneath both, installing GOG and Epic games without a store client.

It is one AppImage. Nothing to install alongside it, and your library, artwork and settings live in a folder beside it — portable, backed up by copying, gone when you delete it.

It found a home

Cafe Neurotico should run on any Linux desktop, but it's being built and tested on Omarchy, with its no-nonsense no BS mentality — which is why it now behaves like part of it rather than a visitor.

In the bar

A widget showing what is installed and what is playing, with a menu one click from the library, the big screen face and your storage.

A launcher overlay: type a few letters, press Enter, play. Cover art, genre and a short description of the game.

Omarchy Themes

It uses the theme Omarchy is using. Install a new one, apply it with omarchy theme set, and Cafe Neurotico is wearing it too.

⚠ Experimental, and honest about it

What you can download today is the 1.12.x experimental line — the work in progress, in fine tuning, not a settled release yet. It is used daily on the machine it is built on, and it will have rough edges. 2.0 is the destination, it's coming soon. If you want the calm version, wait for that one.
