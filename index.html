02_what_it_is.md

One app, two faces

The Manager: the desktop app. Import, organise, scrape, install, launch. Couch Mode: fullscreen and gamepad-first, for the television. Installer: pulls your GOG and Epic games down, no store client.

One AppImage. Library, artwork and settings sit in a folder next to it. Backed up by copying, gone when you delete it.
